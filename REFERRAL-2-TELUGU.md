# Mana Vivaha — Referral Rules (Current)

**Updated: 2026-10-04.** This replaces the older referral proposal that mentioned rewards for any payment, repeat-payment commissions, tier bonuses, and instant payouts. Those are not the current programme rules. The implementation is in `backend/referral.py`; customer terms are shown at `/referral`.

## Referral attachment and credits

A new member must register with a valid referral code attached. On a valid attachment:

- The referrer receives **2 interest-request credits**.
- The new member receives **1 interest-request credit**.
- These credits are not cash, do not unlock contact details, and are separate from the referral reward.

## Possible one-time ₹50 reward

A referrer may qualify for **₹50 once per referred member**, only after that member’s **first qualifying paid consumer-plan purchase**:

- Eligible plan codes: `S_99`, `S_199`, `S_299`, or `S_499`.
- The recorded payment must be at least **₹99** and match the eligible plan’s amount rules.
- The referred member’s later plan purchases do not create another reward.
- Add-ons, renewals, assisted services, boosts, gifts, complimentary grants, B2B/bureau plans, and other non-consumer payments are excluded.

A registration, referral click, or payment for an excluded service is **not** a ₹50 reward. Do not promise earnings before the qualifying payment is confirmed and the reward appears in the referral wallet.

## Payouts and review

- Rewards are recorded in the referral wallet only when the payment qualifies.
- The minimum payout request is **₹100**.
- Payout requests are reviewed manually. A payout is not instant, and processing time or approval is not guaranteed.
- Refunds or chargebacks may reverse a related reward.
- Duplicate, fake, self-referral, spam, or other suspicious patterns may be reviewed. There is no fixed referral-count cap, but every reward remains subject to the eligibility and review rules above.

## Safe, accurate share text

I may qualify once for ₹50 only if your first qualifying paid S_99/S_199/S_299/S_499 consumer plan is at least ₹99. Exclusions apply and payout requests receive manual review. Terms: https://manavivaha.in/referral

Avoid old claims such as “₹50 for every payment,” “repeat 10%,” “everyone gets ₹50,” “instant payout,” or cash rewards for add-ons/renewals. Verify the current terms in the app before publishing any campaign copy.
